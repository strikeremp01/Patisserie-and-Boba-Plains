About Us

The Tsumugi Family Legacy


Three generations ago, our grandmother opened a single wooden counter at the edge of a quiet Kyoto street. She sold only a few things: soft sponge cakes, warm tea, and a smile for every customer who stopped by. She named the shop Tsumugi, after the fragrant flower that blooms with dignity. To her, that meant doing small things with care and not needing to be loud to be remembered.

That idea still guides us. We fold our sponge slowly, we steep our tea patiently, and we never rush the cream. Our mother added the seasonal pastries, like matcha swiss rolls and sakura mont blancs. When the youngest of us came home from studying abroad with a love for boba, we couldn't resist trying chewy brown sugar pearls beside the pastries. It turned out they belong together.

Today, Patisserie & Boba Plains is a small corner shop where pastry and boba share the same warm afternoon. Sit by the window, stay as long as you like, and let the day slow down. We hope that when you leave, you feel the way you would after visiting family.
